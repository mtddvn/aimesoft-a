AimeFace: eKYC, face recognition solution

 

AimeFace is an eKYC (know your customer), face recognition, face registration solution from Aimesoft. It not only supports face recognition, comparison but also Multimodal AI based face registration, face and voice fingerprinting, etc. 

AimeFace Features

  • Recognizing various face at the same time
  • Real-time face recognition, detection, and identification
  • Multimodal AI-based face registration. Allow registering face immediately
  • Real-time model retraining
  • Comparison between actual faces and face images in ID cards
  • Face recognition even in the condition of gentle light
